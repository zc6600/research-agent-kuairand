06 / INSIGHTS

What the run taught us.

Recovery

NumPy float32 values broke serialization after evaluation. The Scientist repaired the writer and retained the evidence.

Audit

Reviews found leakage and sampling mismatches. META scopes evidence; it is not a formal verifier.

Open questions

One task does not establish generality. META, State and reset effects are not isolated. Summaries can lose context and anchor later work.

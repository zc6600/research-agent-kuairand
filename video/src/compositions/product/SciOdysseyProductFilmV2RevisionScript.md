# SciOdyssey Product Film — V2 Revision Script

**Working title:** `The Search Continues`<br>
**Format:** 16:9, 1920×1080, 30fps, 03:00<br>
**Direction:** one continuous white research world; the terminal is a late-stage proof of use, not the opening image.

## Creative corrections

- Remove the cold-open and limitation setup pages. The film starts with the product and its promise.
- Keep one white visual field throughout, matching the existing product presentation language. Dark contrast is reserved for physical terminal objects and deep interface states — never as a full-screen chapter change.
- Introduce the product surface before the workflow: state, evidence, parallel search, and resume.
- Keep the existing keynote as a moving in-world demonstration after the product overview.
- Move the actual terminal walkthrough to the final third, then show resume and the end card.
- Terminals enter with a short deterministic settle and remain level. Camera motion is carried by the world, rails, and screen objects.

## Narrative spine

```text
product promise → product capabilities → keynote proof → parallel search → runtime choice → evidence → how to use → resume → CTA
```

## Editorial rules

1. No full-frame black/white alternation. Keep the camera in the white world and let dark terminal surfaces appear as objects.
2. Every transition is a cyan/violet motion accent, never a white flash.
3. The terminal is a readable interaction surface, not a handheld object: no continuous rotation, spring wobble, or drifting tilt.
4. The product is explained before it is operated. The first explicit command appears in the final third.
5. The old keynote remains a proof point, not the narrative opening.
